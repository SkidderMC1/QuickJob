# EasyJob Product Context (PRODUCT.md)
*Crafted following the Impeccable Frontend Methodology (pbakaus/impeccable)*

---

## 1. Product Truth & Mission
EasyJob (QuickJob) is a mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA) facilitating safe, legal, and rapid micro-job mediation for neighborhood tasks (gardening, tutoring, dog walking, shopping assistance) in Germany.

---

## 2. Core Audiences
1. **Youth Earners (Ages 13–17)**: High school students seeking supplementary pocket money with strict adherence to German youth labor laws (JArbSchG, KindArbSchV, BGB §§ 107, 113).
2. **Adult Neighbors & Hirers (Ages 18+)**: Local residents offering micro-tasks requiring fair pay, rapid booking, and trusted verification.
3. **Guardians & Parents**: Verifying youth tasks, granting electronic consent (PIN-protected), and monitoring micro-job locations.
4. **Platform Compliance Officers / Admins**: Moderating incidents, legal logs, and tax compliance (PStTG threshold tracking).

---

## 3. Statutory & Design Constraints
- **Youth Protection (JArbSchG & KindArbSchV)**: Maximum 2 hours/day on school days, work strictly between 08:00 and 18:00, prohibited categories (heavy lifting, hazardous chemicals, disposal).
- **Privacy & Telemedia (GDPR / TDDDG § 25)**: Opt-in consent banner for storage, zero unauthorized tracking, right to account deletion with escrow safeguard.
- **Accessibility & Design Craft**: WCAG AA color contrast, keyboard navigable dialogs with focus trapping, minimum 44px tap targets, and non-generic tactile UI aesthetics.
